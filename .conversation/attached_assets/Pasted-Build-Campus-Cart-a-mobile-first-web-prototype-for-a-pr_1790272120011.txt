Build “Campus Cart,” a mobile-first web prototype for a private Notre Dame student and staff agentic-commerce experiment. It must open from an iMessage link without installing an app. Use React, a Node backend, and persistent database storage. Make the design clean, fast, and comfortable on iPhone.

Purpose: measure willingness to connect a payment card and discover which items and merchants people want an agent to purchase from.

Build only these three user features:

Speak or type a shopping request.
Provide a large microphone button and a text fallback. Support audio recording and server-side transcription when a transcription key is configured; otherwise retain the text option and explain that voice is unavailable. Let the user edit the transcript. Extract item, merchant if specified, and maximum total budget including taxes and fees. Ask for missing information without inventing merchants or prices. Collect student/staff role and verify an @nd.edu email through an email sign-in link. Use invite codes for private pilot access.

Review the request and connect a card.
Display the request and budget. Clearly state: “This research prototype will save your card through Stripe. No purchase or charge will be made in this session. Future purchases will require separate authorization.” Require explicit consent.
Use Stripe-hosted Checkout with mode=setup. Never collect card numbers or CVC in our app, chat, database, or logs. Store only necessary Stripe identifiers and setup status. Verify setup completion server-side; do not trust the success-page URL. Provide a disconnect-card action.
Default to clearly labeled test mode. If payment credentials are absent, disable card setup with an honest explanation. Never simulate a successful card connection or mix test results with live results.

Show truthful purchase capability and status.
After submission show “Request received—no order placed.” Use a configurable merchant-support list. For unsupported merchants show “We can’t purchase from this merchant yet. Your request has been recorded.” Do not invent purchase attempts, failure reasons, prices, or order confirmations. Keep real purchasing disabled in this version.

Add a password-protected researcher dashboard showing anonymized participant IDs, student/staff role, item, category, requested merchant, budget, card-step views, setup starts, verified setup completions, abandonment, and optional feedback: “What, if anything, stopped you from connecting your card?”
Include CSV export and merchant/category rankings by unique participants. Keep unspecified merchants separate. Label rankings “Requested purchases,” not “Items bought.”

Track funnel events server-side where possible. Separate live and test data. Protect participant records and admin endpoints. Keep credentials in environment secrets and discard raw audio after transcription.

Include a short setup guide for payment, transcription, email, database, and deployment configuration. Include a visible statement that this is an independent research pilot, not an official Notre Dame service. Produce a working prototype, not just a landing page.
